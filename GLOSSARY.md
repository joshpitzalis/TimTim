# Hindi letter practice

Practice writing a prompted Hindi letter and assess the finished drawing.

## Language

**Reference trace**:
The example drawing that defines the target letter form for an exercise. A different acceptable letter form requires its own reference trace.
_Avoid_: Stroke sequence when describing the assessment target

**Letter attempt**:
A learner's complete drawing of one prompted letter, potentially made with several strokes.
_Avoid_: Stroke when referring to the whole letter

**Stroke**:
One uninterrupted drawing gesture, from touching the writing area to lifting the finger or pen.
